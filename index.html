Application guidelines

Before you apply

Read the job description carefully and match your resume to the skills it asks for. Keep your portfolio link short and make sure every project opens without a login.

Tip: send applications early in the week. Recruiters usually review new applications on Monday and Tuesday.

Prepare two or three examples of work you are proud of. Explain what the problem was, what you built, and what you learned from it.

After you apply, check your email and the alerts above every day. Reply to interview invitations within one working day.

Tip: practise answering questions out loud before the interview.
